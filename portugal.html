The pitch

Senior engineering at 40 to 60 percent less

Portugal has one of Europe's strongest tech talent markets. Lisbon and Porto have a dense pool of senior engineers trained in local startups, scaleups, and large consultancies, with English as the working language in most teams.

For a company hiring from London or New York, that means three practical things:

  • Day rates roughly 40 to 60 percent below UK and US equivalents.
  • A working day that overlaps with the UK, Dublin, Berlin, and the US East Coast.
  • EU IP and employment protection, which simplifies the legal and compliance side.
Who we source

Senior specialists, not generalist CVs

We work with engineers, designers, and product people with five years or more of experience, in the specialisms where good talent is hardest to find:

  • Full-stack and backend engineers
  • AI and machine learning engineers
  • DevOps and platform engineers
  • Senior product designers and PMs
  • Web3 and DeFi engineers

Every candidate is vetted by someone who has built and shipped product themselves. If we would not hire them for a Big Room Tech engagement, we will not put them in front of you.
